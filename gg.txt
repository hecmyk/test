Good catch about the inconsistency: _add_usage writes tokens turn after turn, so on a mid-loop crash we ended up with tokens > 0 but n_turns = 0, phase1_time_s = 0.0 and hit_max_iterations = False. Writing those three fields just before the raise would not solve it, because the metrics dict lives in run_agent's frame and is only returned on the success path. When a raise happens, agent_runs.run_documents catches the exception and only records {doc_id, error, result: None}, so the dict is dropped anyway. Two things had to change together: (1) fill the dict even on the failure path (try/finally), and (2) surface it to the caller so it actually lands in agent_runs.json.
